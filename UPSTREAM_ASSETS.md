# Upstream assets and redistribution

This archive contains experiment code, derived LoRA adapters, split metadata,
frozen verifier features, and result audits. It intentionally does not contain
the 16 GB Lingshu-7B base model, the Qwen3-VL semantic judge, or VQA-RAD image
files.

Before redistributing or publishing this archive, the sender and recipient must
review and comply with the licenses and terms of:

- `lingshu-medical-mllm/Lingshu-7B`;
- `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct`;
- VQA-RAD and its underlying medical images;
- all Python dependencies.

The outputs are research benchmark artifacts, not clinical diagnoses or a
medical device.
